Reading and downloading files

“Read HTML” opens the lesson in your browser. “Download” buttons save individual files; if your browser opens a document, use its save command. To work with R, download and extract the entire repository, keeping the original folders. Consult feedback and worked examples after your own attempt.

You do not need R to read HTML and PDF files. The instructor will provide the schedule, submission instructions, and assessment criteria.
